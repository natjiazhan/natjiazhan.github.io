Welcome to my personal website! 

I am an Honors Electrical Engineering Student and President of the Chinese American Student Association at Arizona State University. I am pursuing both my Bachelor's and Master's in Electrical Engineering under the Accelerated Master's program and aim to have both by the end of 2027. 

My interests lie in Digital Design, Computer Architecture, Semiconductors, and Machine Learning. I have industry experience in power delivery as a Transmission Planning Intern at Salt River Project as well as advanced packaging thermal solutions as a Process Integration & Yield Intern at Intel Corporation. Outside of academia, I enjoy weightlifting, cooking, and video editing.
